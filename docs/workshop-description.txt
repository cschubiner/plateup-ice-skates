[h1]Ice Skates[/h1]
Faster chefs, slippery turns, and two fading ice streaks.

Ice Skates adds a hands-free equipment tool with a controllable momentum feel. Skate around tight kitchens, drift through turns, and keep carrying food as usual. The effect belongs only to the chef wearing the skates.

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[list]
[*]A free Ice Skates rack parcel arrives during restaurant prep. Open it before service.
[*]Move the rack normally during prep; it stays fixed during service.
[*]During service or practice, take skates from the rack to equip them. Return them to the rack to unequip.
[*]The rack supplies every chef, but each chef has only one native equipment slot. Remove another equipped tool first.
[*]Skating increases movement force and adds smoothed steering. PlateUp still handles collisions and drag; this is not a separate physics simulation.
[*]Two cosmetic ground streaks follow your blades and fade after you stop. They do not make the floor slippery for other chefs.
[/list]

[h2]Required mods[/h2]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2898069883]KitchenLib[/url]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2898033283]HarmonyX[/url]

Restart PlateUp after subscribing. Do not load both a local Mods-folder copy and the Workshop copy of Ice Skates. For multiplayer, every player should use the same version and dependencies.

[h2]Version and testing[/h2]
Version 0.3.1, checked on PlateUp 1.5.2-7473 with KitchenLib 0.10.1. Includes 42 pure-logic tests, native integration checks, and rendered trail visibility/fade checks. Equipped skating and ground streaks were also verified in a live solo restaurant. Remote and couch co-op have not been comprehensively gameplay-tested.
